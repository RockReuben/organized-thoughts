Working with external intelligence

How I learned to stop worrying and love the bomb

Oct 30th - Nov 14th

I hear a bowling ball totter atop a distant mountain top.

I feel fear motivate a question of what might be, as a puzzle’s pieces fly through the air. I wonder how the pieces will fit together once they’ve settled down.

I find, even now - two years after ChatGPT’s initial demonstration of what the neural net family of models we’ve taken to calling “large language models” are capable of - that I’m as surprised by the variety of their possible applications as I am by the often useful, occasionally brilliant, always instant nature of their responses. As a tool in humanity’s hands, it is the most recent pandora’s box in what has been a matryoshka set of pandora’s boxes - maybe the first was industrialization, or the wheel, or irrigation. Perhaps as time passes we will think of it as being equal to its sisters. Perhaps time will show it to be in a capability class of its own.

What to do, what to do

Of what use is such speculation at the moment? I’ve to deduce what it means for me, what I should do about a tool that some say can be a superpower in the hands of a software engineer; what exactly this technology upheaves, what it obfuscates, what it deletes, and what it constructs - for the software engineer, and for any who seek to see new software running in the world.

Room for Us in the Process?

Since there are so many possible answers in this line of questioning - of what AI upheaves, obfuscates, deletes, and constructs for the software engineer - I will wing this part.

The software is here in the first place to make things easier, and so the software architect, builder, and tester (what will hereafter be termed ‘engineer’) is only here to make software’s construction and function easier.

So when and how to use a magic box that takes in words and can generate system subcomponents?

The construction could be done without checking the magic box’s work - if one was sure the magic box can always make that type of subcomponent correctly. For any system/subsystem whose continual functioning is important, it must be fixable by humans in order to be maximally useful. In order for it to be fixable by humans it must be human-explainable, human-checkable, human-fixable work. Here is where justification for the human place in the creation process may be found.

Justification, ok, but what exactly is that place going forward? Surely it shouldn’t be the same as it was before, when so many essential functions of the engineer were only doable by the engineer themself or another trained craftsman.

This is where I’m leaving it for today. Where is the software engineer’s place in software engineering going forward?
